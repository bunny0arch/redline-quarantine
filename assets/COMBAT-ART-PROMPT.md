# Combat atlas prompt
Built-in image_gen, one generation, 2026-09-19. Used as fallback for missing combat assets, not for model furniture. Project asset: assets/facility/combat-atlas.png.

Create ONE production game sprite atlas image with a genuinely transparent alpha background, 1536 by 1536 square, exactly 3 columns and 3 rows of equal 512px square cells. Nine separate isolated sprites, one centered in each cell, generous transparent padding, no borders, no labels, no text, no checkerboard drawn. Realistic hand-painted gritty 2D overhead survival shooter asset style, fine material texture, desaturated cold industrial colors. Camera STRICTLY directly ABOVE looking DOWN at top of heads and shoulders, NOT side view, NOT isometric standing portraits. Characters crouch slightly in combat. All four characters face EAST / right edge of image so they can be rotated by the engine. Scale bodies to fit 330x330px inside own cells, boss 410x410. Row1 col1: human survivor wearing weathered olive tactical jacket and backpack, brown hair, no helmet, empty hands forward. Row1 col2: infected hostile humanoid with torn brown overalls, gray-green skin and hooked forearms, overhead visible crown of head and shoulders. Row1 col3: grotesque hulking mutant boss with asymmetric armored shoulders, exposed dark red organic growths and long sinewy claw arms, distinct detailed silhouette, top down facing right. Row2 col1: cured companion, tired human with desaturated teal worn clothes and empty hands, overhead facing right. Row2 col2: realistic worn black compact pistol viewed from its side as an inventory item, muzzle points right, no hands. Row2 col3: small dark brass cartridge box with six visible individual rounds, viewed from above. Row3 col1: battered olive fabric medical pouch with an ivory plus patch, viewed from above. Row3 col2: single violet glass antidote vial with metal cap, no text. Row3 col3: small pile of rusty metal scraps, gears and washers, viewed from above. Neutral soft lighting from upper left, dimensional highlights and self-shadows only, NO cast floor shadows or environment, NO scene, NO UI. Keep every sprite entirely contained within its cell and disconnected from the others. This is a functional atlas for a dark 2D game, not a concept art board.

Actual output: 1254 by 1254 RGBA; renderer computes cell sizes from the real image dimensions, not the requested resolution.
